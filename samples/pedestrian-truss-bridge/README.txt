PEDESTRIAN TRUSS BRIDGE - CONCEPTUAL DESIGN STUDY (FrameLytix educational sample)

This folder is an ORIGINAL, FICTIONAL educational sample project bundled with
FrameLytix. It is not a real project, does not describe a real site, and was not
produced by a practising engineer.

All numerical values (4.0 kPa pedestrian load, 150 MPa allowable stress, member
geometry, scores, risk ratings) are ILLUSTRATIVE ASSUMPTIONS chosen to demonstrate
the software. They are not taken from, or checked against, any design code.

NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION. Do not use any drawing, model, calculation or conclusion in
this folder for design, fabrication or construction.

Concept geometry: Pratt through truss, clear span 24.0 m (6 panels x 4.0 m), truss
depth 3.0 m, two trusses at 2.9 m centres, clear deck width 2.5 m, deck at bottom
chord, floor beams at each panel point, top lateral bracing.

Open framelytix-project.json with FrameLytix (File > Open Project) to explore the
requirements, engineering arguments, decision matrices, calculations, risks and
traceability. Several items are deliberately left incomplete or unverified to show
how FrameLytix highlights gaps.

The sample files are generated by scripts/generate-sample-assets.mjs and
scripts/build-sample-manifest.mjs in the FrameLytix repository.
